## What Is It?

Forevercraft gives vanilla *depth*. A reason to keep mining after diamonds. A reason to fish in the rain. A reason to walk into a structure you've seen a hundred times and feel something new.

At its core is the **Dream Rate** — a custom luck system that ties your entire journey together. The artifacts you wear, the companions you raise, the constellations you complete, the time of day, even the phase of the moon all feed into it. Start at zero. The world opens as you do.

**The Bountiful Harvest Expansion** is our biggest content wave yet — a cozy-but-deep update built around *gathering, growing, building, and collecting*. A whole new Gatherer's Path, antique furniture, structure-wide boons, a learns-your-trash spirit bag, and a Dream Rate ceiling raised from 50 all the way to **100**.

### ⭐ The Backbone

<details>
<summary><b>✦ DREAM RATE</b> — the luck system everything feeds into</summary>

<br>

Dream Rate **is** your luck — a single universal attribute woven into every system in the pack. It rises from artifacts, companions, constellations, journals, cooking buffs, guild perks, the time of day, the moon, and dozens of other sources, all summed together. The higher it climbs, the rarer the loot the world hands you.

It gates the best crates — **Ornate at DR 5, Exquisite at DR 15, Mythical at DR 25** — and the structure ladder opens as you rise: shipwrecks, then villages, ruins, pyramids, fortresses, strongholds, ancient cities. Reach the cap to become a **Dream Transcendent**.

The cap was raised from 50 to **100** in the Bountiful Harvest update; DR 50 is now just the final *named* milestone, with a whole second climb beyond it.

<details>
<summary>🔒 Spoiler — what waits at the top</summary>

<br>

A separate **Dream Rank** ladder (G → F → E → D → C → B → A → S → SS → **SSS**) runs off lifetime Dream Points and unlocks housing slots and server-wide broadcasts. Past **DR 30**, the new moon stops being safe — see *Night Terrors*. Crossing DR 30 for the very first time, *anywhere on the server*, triggers a one-time world event called **The Dreaming**.

</details>

</details>

### ⚔️ Artifacts & Gear

<details>
<summary><b>⚔️ ARTIFACTS</b> — 600+ handcrafted items, not enchantments</summary>

<br>

Over **600** unique artifacts — weapons, armor, accessories, and tools — each with bespoke abilities scripted by hand rather than borrowed from vanilla enchantments. They span **6 rarity tiers**: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Ornate, Exquisite, and **Mythical**.

Weapons sort into combat classes (Striker maces, Berserker axes, Dancer gauntlets, Beastlord spears, Archer bows, Rogue daggers, Healer support tools, and more), each scaling from Common to Mythical. Mythical tools add real quality-of-life power: vein-mining pickaxes, tree-felling axes, item and XP magnets. They drop from crates, treasure, structure bosses, the gacha fountain, profession trials, and questline finales — with the rarest tiers gated behind your Dream Rate.

<details>
<summary>🔒 Spoiler — endgame artifacts</summary>

<br>

Mythical capstones include the **Codex of Everything** (dimensional teleport), the **Dragon Fan** (unlocks a hidden combat class), and the **Ender Dragon Blade**. An 11-family **fusion lattice** at the Tinkerer's Plinth combines lesser pieces up into "Grand" capstones like the Dreamer's Reliquary, Heart of the Mountain, and Cornucopia Tide.

</details>

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🛡️ ARMOR SETS</b> — 28 themed sets with stacking bonuses</summary>

<br>

**28** themed armor sets, from early Blood and Frost plate all the way to Ender Dragon and Titan. Every set grants a **2-piece** and **4-piece** bonus, and the mythical sets push further with larger multi-piece ultimates and weapon cross-synergies. Crystal raises a prism shield, Titan lets you walk on water, Shadow grants assassinate strikes, and Splendid's compass locates 13 different structure types once a day.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>✨ TRIMS & GLYPHS</b> — armor trims, materials, and the Glyphforge</summary>

<br>

Three layers of customization stack on your gear:

- **18 trim patterns** — each grants a passive while you wear one piece and a dramatic ultimate at four. A full Flow set summons an invisible wind-horse; Silence grants a sonic-boom "Haunted Bow"; Wild calls a three-wolf pack.
- **11 trim materials** — rewards the *metal* the trim is forged from: Netherite floats and softens landings, Emerald grants true dodges, Diamond scales companion damage, Amethyst boosts Dream Rate and mining speed.
- **The Glyphforge** — a lodestone forge where runes crystallize over real time into **13 glyphs**, each chosen as one of three facets (⚔ Offensive / 🛡 Defensive / ✦ Passive) at the moment you collect it — 39 distinct effects in all.
- **Inscription** — project glyph-stone auras into the world (life-steal, thornmail, growth, haste); place two specific stones together to form a combined **Rune Word**.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🕰️ PATINA</b> — artifacts that age with use</summary>

<br>

The artifacts you actually use visibly age, gaining new lore lines as they pass through **Worn → Seasoned → Storied**. At the final **Legendary** stage they earn a permanent **+2%** bonus — attack damage for weapons, max health for armor — so a well-loved artifact is measurably stronger than a fresh one.

</details>

Summon them, feed them, gift them, and send them on expeditions. Every duplicate you collect *fuses* into that specific companion for a permanent power bump and advances its evolution.

### 🗡️ Classes, Combat & Mastery

<details>
<summary><b>⚡ CLASSES & AFFINITY</b> — 21 playable classes decided by what's in your hands</summary>

<br>

You don't pick a class from a menu — your class is decided by **what you're holding**. Grab a sword-type artifact and you're a Berserker; pick up a hoe and you're a Growseer. There are **21 playable classes**: **14 combat** (Rogue, Berserker, Dancer, Striker, Sentinel, Healer, Beastlord, Javelin, Hoplite, Archer, Hunter, Knight, Tank, and one more) and **7 crafting** (Stonestrike, Lumberfell, Growseer, Terrawarp, Sirencall, Lamentor, Gearwright).

Every class earns **Affinity** as you use its gear, climbing **7 stages** that raise that class's output up to **+75%** (and beyond when twin-wielding a spirit weapon). Gains are daily-capped, so mastery grows over weeks — and there's no class-lock, so you can master all 21.

<details>
<summary>🔒 Spoiler — the hidden 14th combat class</summary>

<br>

The fourteenth combat class — the **Dual Swordsman** — appears in no menu and no codex. It awakens only when you hold a specific spirit dual-sword in your main hand and any sword in your off-hand; until then, its entire UI stays hidden so it never spoils itself. It's a composite "everything" class, inheriting Tank's bonus health, Knight's reach, Dancer's speed, a refreshing absorption shield, and a Whirlwind passive — and twin-wielding the matched pair scales your attack speed up to 10×.

</details>

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🌳 SKILL TREES</b> — 15 Advantage trees + a Tree for all 21 classes</summary>

<br>

Two separate progression systems:

- **15 Advantage Trees** — account-wide, filled passively by playing (walking levels Agility, mining levels Mining, cooking levels Culinary). Each has **25 levels**; max one out and you can **Prestige** it across **3 cycles**, each keeping a slice of peak power. Trees unlock **45 prestige abilities** like Dash, Double Jump, Ore Magnet, and Death Save.
- **The Tree (per-class)** — *new this update* — every one of the 21 classes has its own skill tree. Using a class's gear earns Mastery XP and Bough Points to spend on nodes, with a slice of every gain cross-training your other classes. Top nodes unlock a Crown and signature mastery moves.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📿 MASTERY & MORALITY</b> — weapons, armor, biomes, and the Reaper</summary>

<br>

- **Weapon Mastery** — 13 weapon types each level independently for tier-scaled enchantments; awaken them with Awakening Stones and Prestige at max to climb again.
- **Armor Mastery** — all 4 armor slots gain their own mastery from wear and absorbed damage.
- **Biome Mastery** — master 25 biomes across 5 stages each for titles, damage reduction, and home-turf gathering bonuses.

<details>
<summary>🔒 Spoiler — the Reaper (morality)</summary>

<br>

Your deeds set a moral alignment. **Infamy** rises when you kill villagers and iron golems — push it high and escalating **Hunting Parties** come for you. **Renown** rises through peaceful trade and good deeds — but a rich reputation makes you a target for **Bandit raids**. Both decay over time. Neither path is permanent; your choices shape how the world responds.

</details>

</details>

### 💀 Endgame

<details>
<summary><b>🗡️ SPIRIT WEAPONS</b> — 14 soulbound legendaries with 7-tier metamorphosis</summary>

<br>

Forevercraft's flagship endgame weapons: **14** unique soulbound blades, axes, spears, and shields — one tuned to each combat archetype. Each ascends through **7 tiers** (Common → … → Mythical → **Spirit**), carries two signature abilities plus a tier-scaling passive, survives death, and can be traded between players. Reaching the final Spirit tier demands a grueling seven-requirement **Metamorphosis**.

<details>
<summary>🔒 Spoiler — twin forms & metamorphosis</summary>

<br>

Seven of the fourteen weapons hide a **Twin** form, revealed by consuming a **Dream Storm Crystal** (Firebrand ⇄ Aqualoch, Hollow Fangs ⇄ Ghost Fang, Dragonheart ⇄ Johan Shield, and four more). Hold both twins and they unlock their true power. The other seven instead gain a solo power boost from the crystal. The Spirit-tier metamorphosis requires all glyphs at Exquisite, 100,000 mastery XP, and **all 13 raid bosses slain**, plus four weapon-specific trials.

</details>

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>💀 RAID BOSSES & WORLD BOSSES</b> — 13 raid bosses, 11 roaming titans</summary>

<br>

- **Spirit Raids** — a 10-floor co-op gauntlet you descend into by party vote. Fight five mob waves per floor to the bottom, where one of **13** multi-phase raid bosses waits. Each scales to your party size and Dream Rate, with three phases, puzzles, adds, and anti-cheese pull mechanics. Victory grants an Exquisite artifact; defeat wipes your XP levels.
- **World Bosses** — **11** roaming overworld titans that spawn naturally every few in-game days (or via a Totem of Summoning). Four HP-gated phases plus a final Rage state, telegraphed attacks, and seasonal/lunar buffs (a Blood Moon makes them far deadlier). Kill thresholds award escalating Slayer titles.

<details>
<summary>🔒 Spoiler — once-per-server glory</summary>

<br>

Each raid boss carries a permanent **"Realm First!"** announcement for whoever fells it first on the server.

<details>
<summary><b>🏰 INFINITE CASTLE</b> — an endless scaling dungeon</summary>

<br>

Climb as high as you can; death ends the run. Mobs scale relentlessly per floor, by your Dream Rate, and by party size. A boss arrives **every 10th floor** — and they *stack*, so floor 100 fields ten simultaneous bosses. Your highest floor is permanent. Entry costs keys, with three runs a day.

<details>
<summary>🔒 Spoiler — what's deep in the castle</summary>

<br>

Fixed mini-bosses guard the early depths (the Iron Phalanx on floor 20 drops a spirit weapon at 5%; the Crimson Bulwark on floor 25). **Dream Storm Crystals** — the item that reveals spirit-weapon twins — begin dropping at floor 26. The floor-100 capstone hands out a Dreamy Star, Mythical Awakening Stones, the **Lord** title, and more.

</details>

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🔑 DUNGEONS & HEIST</b> — instanced runs at every structure</summary>

<br>

Use a **Dungeon Key** at any of 17 detectable structure types (or a Defense Horn at a village) to spawn a 5-wave instanced dungeon with one of **16 rotating themes**. Clear waves, descend up to 10 floors, and earn Dream-Rate-scaled crates; floor 10 hands off to a Spirit Raid. Difficulty scales by tier, Dream Rate, floor, and party size, and party runs add random affixes (Glass Cannon, Darkness, and more). A daily challenge rotates the featured structure for free entry. The **Heist** system adds solo stealth infiltrations of the Black Market for rare loot.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>💎 SALVATION STONE</b> — the rarest item in the game</summary>

<br>

A consumable death-protection charge — and the single rarest item in Forevercraft. It is **never a random drop**; it's *earned*, granted one-time for a handful of legendary lifetime feats (first Ender Dragon kill, all constellations, Dream Rate 50, Castle floor 100, all spirit weapons metamorphosed) and for the finales of the pack's longest questlines.

<details>
<summary>🔒 Spoiler — what it does</summary>

<br>

Right-click to consume it and bank a death-protection charge. On your next death it auto-spends: in **Normal** mode you lose nothing — no tomb, no XP loss, full inventory kept. In **Pioneer** hardcore mode, your stats and progression survive the wipe even though your items don't. *"Your legacy endures. Your belongings do not."* Charges stack.

</details>

Beyond the board run **seven dedicated questline "tomes"**: the Adventurer's, Chemist's, Smith's, Cook's, Tinkerer's, and Handcrafter's paths, plus the sprawling **Angler's Tome** — a 250-stage fishing journey all its own. ### 🍳 Cooking & The Gatherer's Path

<details>
<summary><b>🍳 COOKING</b> — 12 cuisine categories with a skill mini-game</summary>

<br>

Cook themed meals at a **Campfire Kitchen** across **12 cuisine categories** (Combat, Mining, Fortune, Wayfarer, Delicacy, Seasonal, Treats, Rations, Feast, Preserve, Spirit, and Spiced), each granting buffs in its domain. Per-category mastery unlocks stronger Hearty → Gourmet → Feast recipe tiers, recipes are discovered as you cook, and a **Pantry** block auto-feeds ingredients. The **Chef's Touch** is a three-phase skill mini-game whose quality (Bland → Masterwork) is stamped onto the dish — and **Preserved** meals keep their buffs right through death. Seasonal recipes rotate with the four seasons.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🌿 THE GATHERER'S PATH</b> — a unified harvest-skill engine (NEW)</summary>

<br>

The headline feature of the Bountiful Harvest update. A shared harvest engine spans **7 node families**: ore **Prospecting**, **Forage** bushes, fishing **Splash-Nodes**, **Nests**, **Chest-Nodes**, **Timberfall** chopping, and **Buried Caches**. Each spawns a node you interact with, and the engine rolls a single RuneScape-style success check that scales with your crafting class's level versus the node's tier (you can hit 100% with a spirit tool). Nodes hold a couple of charges and crumble after repeated failures; every outcome pays class XP, and wins can trickle Forever Coins, bonus crates, and the **Dreamy Pull** mini-game.

<details>
<summary>🔒 Spoiler — Dreamy Pull, festivals & co-op</summary>

<br>

**Dreamy Pull** is a bonus rhythm/timing mini-game (three styles) that fires on a successful harvest — nail it for extra rare loot like glyphs, reagents, or bonus crates. The seasonal **Node Festival** features one node family per day across five days; play three of them for a Mythical-artifact capstone. **Co-op Node Events** set a server-wide shared gathering goal that every contributor cashes in on.

</details>

### 🔨 CraftForever & The Forge

<details>
<summary><b>🔨 CRAFTFOREVER</b> — Artisan Rank, the Forge, and craft mastery</summary>

<br>

The crafting-side counterpart to combat progression. **Artisan Rank (1–100)** is earned from every gather, cook, forge, build, catch, and brew, and feeds a passive **Craft Rate** multiplier on yields and quality. **7 craft mastery tracks** (Mining, Cooking, Fishing, Building, Foraging, Forging, Alchemy) each climb their own ladder.

The **Artisan Forge** — a lodestone station unlocked at Artisan Rank 5 — refines **15 biome materials** gathered across the world into **28 artifact-grade components spanning 7 tiers**, then fuses them with catalysts into weapons, armor, tools, and accessories. The **Fusion Funnel** runs a four-phase alchemy mini-game from four reagent families.

<details>
<summary>🔒 Spoiler — Spirit Tools & the Grand Forge</summary>

<br>

**6 Spirit Tools** — one per craft discipline (Earthsong pickaxe, Bloomweaver hoe, Tidecaller rod, and three more) — drop at 5% from a Tier-10 Craft Trial and ascend through seven tiers along a 100-part Artisan Tome. The **Grand Forge** is the capstone: a five-phase craft gauntlet ignited by a rare Forge Catalyst, whose first clear yields the permanent **Forge Crystal**.

</details>

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🏅 CRAFT TRIALS</b> — 6 disciplines × 10 tiers</summary>

<br>

Instanced solo puzzle-arenas across **6 disciplines** — Mining, Farming, Fishing, Building, Lumber, and Artisan — each with **10 escalating tiers**. Spend a Trial Pass or run the free daily challenge. **Hard Mode** halves your clock and saddles you with Mining Fatigue for +50% XP. Clear all ten tiers of a discipline for its master title; conquer all six to become a **Grand Master**.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>⚙️ TINKERER'S PLINTH</b> — fuse accessories into Spirit Artifacts (NEW)</summary>

<br>

A data-driven fusion altar: place a Plinth on a beacon and feed it compatible trinkets to fuse them into a single capstone accessory. **11 fusion families** (56 recipes) culminate in grand "Spirit Artifacts" — the Pebble of Creation, Band of All Rings, Dreamer's Reliquary, Heart of the Mountain, and more — and a 12-step quest line ends in the **Master Tinkerer** title. The companion **Hand of Creation** is a balance-neutral builder's fusion that frees up accessory slots. *"You have not gained power; you have gained room."*

</details>
