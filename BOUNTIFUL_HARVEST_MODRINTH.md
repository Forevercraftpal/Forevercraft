# 🌾 Forevercraft — The Bountiful Harvest Expansion

> *Every world has treasure. This one has dreams.*

A complete fantasy overhaul of vanilla Minecraft — **150+ interconnected systems, one datapack, free forever.** At its core is the **Dream Rate**: a custom luck attribute fed by everything you wear, raise, and discover. The higher it climbs, the more the world opens up.

**The Bountiful Harvest Expansion** is our biggest wave yet — built around gathering, growing, building, and collecting.

## ✨ What's New

- 🌿 **The Gatherer's Path** — a skill-based harvest engine across **7 node families** (prospecting, forage, fishing, nests, chest-nodes, timber, buried caches)
- 🎣 **Dreamy Pull** — a bonus mini-game that fires on lucky harvests
- 🪑 **Antique Furniture** — quality grades (Poor / Standard / **Pristine**) earned through a sawmill skill game, with hidden material infusions

- 📖 **Pioneer Codex Vault** — enshrine artifacts that survive even a hardcore death
- 🐾 **Companion Catalogue** — track all **104** companions + golden shinies, with duplicate-fusion
- 🌳 **The Tree** — a skill tree for every one of the **21** classes
- 🌒 **Dream Rate cap raised 50 → 100**, gacha rebalanced, fresh seasonal resource-pack pass
